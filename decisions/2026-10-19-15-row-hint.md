# 15. Stage 2: the wording of the per-row hint

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Status:** the repo author's wording, approved in chat on 2026-10-19.
  How stage 2 is run (its own run, or a third request in stage 1) is not yet
  decided.

## Context

A one-session smoke test of two hints on file e
(`experiments/smoke-2026-10-18-row-hints/`) showed both taken up. The repo
author wanted a hint that generalizes beyond spreadsheets' rows. In
spike-32, sessions given checksum wordings without "use scripts" made
per-row checksums unprompted in 24 of 40 sessions (all typed, none
computed; two included one memorised value, the checksum of empty text),
and fix-line sessions in none of 20; so the hint is less likely to teach
something new than to bring forward an option sessions already know, in the
sessions that run code.

## Decision

The hint, added after the fix line:

> A checksum can cover smaller pieces of a file, like a single row or cell,
> not just the whole file.

## Consequences

- Stage 2 measures both what the hint should help (real per-row or per-cell
  checksums in sessions that run code) and what it might worsen (invented
  checksums in sessions that type the sheet).
- Not yet tried in a session.
