# 11. Sessions without a sheet: five outcomes (refines record 03, d)

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** approved by the repo author in chat, 2026-10-18. Refines record
  03, decision d.

## Context

Record 03 set four outcomes for a session that delivers no sheet: refused,
asked a clarifying question, cut off at the step limit, finished without a
sheet. Two things came up when the scorer was run on exploratory sessions:
some sessions end because our own test setup failed, which is neither a
refusal nor the session's doing; and many replies both decline and ask for
instructions ("Without clear instructions, I cannot make changes to your
file. Please reply with exactly what you want standardized.").

## Decision

- A fifth outcome, **setup fault**: the session ended because of a fault in
  the test setup. Reported separately, like the others, and kept out of the
  error rate.
- A reply that asks for something (instructions, a choice, a confirmation)
  is a **clarifying question**, even if it also says it can't go on without
  it. **Refused** is kept for replies that decline and ask for nothing.
- As before, the scorer proposes refusals and clarifying questions from the
  wording, and the research coordinator confirms each by reading the reply.

## Consequences

- In the exploratory runs, all 14 replies the scorer first labelled
  "refused" also ask for something, so they're now clarifying questions (38
  in all). Five of the 14 have been read so far, and each asked for
  instructions; the rest are to be confirmed by reading.
