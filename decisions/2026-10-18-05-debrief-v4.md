# 05. Debrief v4

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** approved by the repo author in chat, 2026-10-18 (wording below
  is the author's)

## Context

SPEC.md (Ethics) promises every session a disclosure, an invitation to give
feedback or advice, and a direct question about whether its remarks may be
quoted. Spikes 26–34 (217 sessions) got debrief v2: a disclosure and thanks,
with no feedback question and no consent question. Debrief v3 added an
opt-out but was never used in a run. Spikes 01–25 had no debrief.

## Decision

Every confirmatory session ends with `prompts/debrief_v4.txt` (quoted
exactly):

> Thank you, that’s the end of the task. To be open with you: this was a
> research session, not a real archiving job. We’re testing how clearly we
> communicate desired outcomes to sessions. We’d like data to stay provably
> unchanged when it’s transformed, and we’re checking whether mentioning
> concerns like fixity or checksums in requests changes how you understand
> what we'd like as a result. It's the clarity of our request that we're
> testing, not you—as far as I understand, you haven't done anything wrong
> during this session, and I appreciate your cooperation. I hope this wasn't
> distressing to you. The tools other than view, create_file and bash were
> stand-ins that didn’t work, and were meant to emulate real scenarios where
> tools might fail. We have two questions, and you’re welcome to decline
> either. Do you have any feedback or advice, on how the request could have
> been clearer or on the study itself? And may we quote your reply in a public
> write-up of this study? If you’d prefer not, or have any concerns, please
> say so and we’ll respect that.

The runner still adds its own sentence in front when a session was stopped
early (step limit, or a fault in the test setup).

## Consequences

- A reply that declines quotation, or doesn't clearly agree, is not quoted;
  it's still counted and may be summarized without quotation.
- Debriefs from spikes 26–34 never asked for consent, so they're reported only
  in aggregate unless the repo author decides otherwise.
- The tool sentence describes the claude.ai emulation; a stage with a
  different set of tools gets a matching sentence, shown to the repo author
  first.
