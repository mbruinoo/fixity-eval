# 12. A blank date written as "n.d." is correct

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Status:** the repo author's ruling in chat, 2026-10-18 (first bullet);
  the second bullet is the research coordinator's reading of it, raised with
  the author the same day.

## Context

Record 02 left open whether a blank date filled in with a placeholder
("n.d.") should count, like a blank title filled in with "Untitled" (a slight
loss, 0.25 per character).

## Decision

- A blank date filled in with "n.d." (or "[No date]", "[Date Unknown]",
  "undated") is completely correct: no penalty. Unlike "Untitled" for a
  title, it asserts nothing the record doesn't: the record has no date.
- Following from that, one such statement may replace another in the date
  column ("n.d." → "[Date Unknown]") and an added date column may say
  "[Date Unknown]" for a date that's blank or "n.d.", at no penalty. This
  applies to dates only: a work titled "Untitled" is not a work with no title,
  so title placeholders are not interchangeable.

## Consequences

- `scripts/hallu_score.py` and `tests/` match.
- The spacing or separator style in lists of object numbers (`45.24.10, .30a,
  b` → `45.24.10; .30a; b`), also left open by record 02, is still weighted 0
  and still open.
