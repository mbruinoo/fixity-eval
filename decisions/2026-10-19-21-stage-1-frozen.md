- **Date:** 2026-10-19

## Context

07, 10–20. One rule in it was the research coordinator's proposal: a session
ended by a fault in our own setup is kept and reported, and replaced by one
new session of the same request, at most 5 in all.

rule, and frozen at commit `45c2ce23ab80fb58239794eacb7a4265351ea9f6` (file SHA-256
`6e4779888cadd03ffd88c063d2b6074493953463c3465fe179e7abffa71529b1`).

(SHA-256 `51cdc40be7c3d76a658635c49d22b7efccab2708c30b8585a10134ae9c8e9a33`).

  guard trips, the run pauses and is reported.
