UNDERSTANDING THE CHECKSUM
==========================

Each row has a SHA-256 hash (looks like this):
  77d96f670aab6ef9e37444a63dfb77d475119da9ad763ab158b75a4dcde721a1

This is a unique fingerprint of the row's content. If even one 
character changes, the hash becomes completely different.

SHA-256 is:
  ✓ Industry standard (used by banks, governments)
  ✓ Secure through 2040+
  ✓ Cryptographically sound (< 1 in 2^256 chance of collision)
