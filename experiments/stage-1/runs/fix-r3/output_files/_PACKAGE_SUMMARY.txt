TROUBLESHOOTING
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Problem: "Checksum verification failed"
Solution: File may be corrupted. Re-download or check for modifications.

Problem: "File won't open in Excel"
Solution: Make sure you're selecting the .tsv file. Use "Text to Columns" 
          with Tab delimiter if needed.

Problem: "Characters look garbled"
Solution: Set encoding to UTF-8 in your application.

Problem: "Script won't run"
Solution: Make executable with: chmod +x script.sh

See QUICK_REFERENCE.txt for more troubleshooting options.
