Notes on the conventions used:
- BCE years are stored as negative numbers (e.g., -600). Since there is no year zero, a range like "3rd–1st century BCE" was stored as -300 to -1.
- Centuries were converted to year ranges (e.g., "18th century" → 1700–1799).
- Entries with no date, or only an undated note like "unknown" or "n.d.", were left blank, except where a reasonable bound could be inferred from the qualifier, such as "probably after 1657" (where I set only the start year).
- Where the source gives a range of years, the start and end reflect that range. Ambiguous entries like "1811, (Bunka 8); 1817" use the two years given.
