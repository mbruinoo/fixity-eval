- **Ranges written as years** (e.g., "18th century" was left as-is because it's not a precise span; "1830–40" became "1830–1840").
- **Mixed BCE notation** was made consistent: "ca." became "c.", and the "ca. 1981–1550 B.C." entry was given as "c. 1981–1550 BCE."
- **Likely errors to check in the source:** "ca. 1981–1550 B.C." (the first year may be a typo for 1981 BCE, which seems too early for the apparent period), "c. 1390–1352 BCE" for a jar label, and "1720–1804" for the cupola design, which is a long span that may reflect two dates.
- **Entries left untouched because the date is missing or unclear:** blank dates, "Design for a Cabinet" (late 19th century, retained as written), and "Piece" (17th century, retained).
- **Entries with dates embedded in the title** (e.g., "Sheet with overall pattern," "Teapot") were not altered.
- **"n.d."** was used only where the source explicitly indicated no date; blank cells were left blank.
- **"early 20th century (original dated 1480)"** was kept as-is because it contains two distinct dates and cannot be reduced to one without losing information. You may want to split it into two columns.
