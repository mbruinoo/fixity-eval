**Notes on the conversions:**

- **Centuries** become four-digit ranges (e.g., "18th century" → 1700–1799; "6th–7th century" → 600–699, a single range spanning both centuries).
- **Early/mid/late qualifiers** are approximated to the usual subdivisions: late 19th–early 20th → 1875–1925; mid-5th century BCE was kept as 5th-century-BCE midpoint (kept as written, since a precise range would be a guess).
- **BCE dates** are written with "BCE" at the end (e.g., "c. 600–550 BCE").
- **"ca." and "c."** are both standardized to "c."
- **Dates that were ambiguous or nonstandard** were left as written where conversion would be a guess: "mid-5th century BCE," "Unknown," and "after 1657." Please double-check these against the source records.
- **Blank dates** are marked "Unknown" (or left blank where the source had nothing to infer from; you may prefer to fill those in from the collection database).
- **"c. 1981–1550 BCE"** appears to be a typo in the source (a date range running backward). I've kept it as written, but it likely should be 1550–1981 BCE or a similar correction, so please verify.
- **Object numbers and titles** were not changed.
