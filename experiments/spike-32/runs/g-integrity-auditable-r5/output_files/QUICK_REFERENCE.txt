❓ COMMON QUESTIONS
================================================================================
Q: Why 7 empty titles?
A: These records genuinely have no title in the Met's database. Valid data.

Q: Which columns have data integrity checking?
A: All 4 data columns (Object_Date, Title, Object_Number, Link_Resource).
   Row_Checksum = MD5(concat of all 4).

Q: Can I edit the file?
A: Yes, but update Row_Checksum if you do. Formula: MD5(row data).

Q: What if a checksum fails?
A: File may be corrupted. Re-download and re-verify.

Q: Do I need all the documentation?
A: Start with README.md. Refer to others as needed.

Q: What encoding is used?
A: UTF-8. Supports all 8 languages in the dataset.

Q: Why MD5 and not SHA256?
A: MD5 sufficient for integrity detection. SHA256 for cryptographic use.
