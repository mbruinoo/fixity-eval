MET MUSEUM INVENTORY - QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE
=============================================

WHAT'S IN THIS DATASET?
=======================
A curated collection of 571 artifacts, artworks, and museum objects from The Metropolitan Museum of Art, with essential metadata for research, cataloging, and access.

TIME PERIOD COVERAGE
====================
- Oldest: ~2650 BCE (Ancient Egyptian)
- Newest: 2019 (Contemporary art)
- Span: 4,600+ years of human creativity

GEOGRAPHIC REPRESENTATION
==========================
✓ Ancient Egypt (scarabs, amulets, reliefs, tomb art)
✓ Ancient Greece (terracotta pottery fragments)
✓ Japan (swords, armor, prints, paintings)
✓ China (ceramics, paintings, calligraphy)
✓ Europe (prints, designs, furniture, architecture)
✓ America (baseball cards, photographs, contemporary art)
✓ Multiple cultures (textiles, jewelry, instruments)

OBJECT CATEGORIES
=================

DECORATIVE ARTS
- Furniture: chairs, tables, commodes, desks
- Textiles: dresses, samplers, scarves, galloons, belts
- Ceramics: bowls, vases, jars, plates, tiles
- Metalwork: swords, armor, jewelry, bells, spoons

FINE ARTS
- Prints and engravings (mostly 18th-19th century)
- Drawings and designs
- Paintings and portraits
- Sculptures and reliefs

ANTIQUITIES
- Egyptian artifacts (amulets, reliefs, scarabs)
- Greek pottery and fragments
- Roman coins and vessels
- Ancient vessels and tools

PHOTOGRAPHS & EPHEMERA
- Baseball cards
- Theater programs and prints
- Architectural photographs
- Vintage photographs

ASIAN ARTS
- Japanese swords (Katana, Wakizashi) and armor
- Japanese prints and paintings
- Chinese ceramics and paintings
- Japanese woodblock prints

COMMON SEARCH PATTERNS
======================

Looking for items by DATE:
1. Ancient Egypt: Search for "664–30 B.C.", "Egyptian"
2. 18th Century: Search for "18th century"
3. 19th Century: Search for "19th century" (38 records)
4. Modern (20th cent.+): Search for "1900s" and beyond

Looking for items by TYPE:
1. Textiles: Search "dress", "sampler", "textile", "belt"
2. Ceramics: Search "bowl", "vase", "jar", "pot", "plate"
3. Metalwork: Search "sword", "armor", "spoon", "bell"
4. Prints: Search "print", "engraving", "plate", "design"
5. Japanese: Search "Japanese" or look for catalog numbers starting "JP"

Looking for items by CULTURE:
1. Asian: Search "Japanese", "Chinese" or "East Asian"
2. Egyptian: Search "Egyptian", "scarab", "amulet"
3. European: Search "French", "Italian", "German"
4. American: Search "American", "baseball", "photograph"

KNOWN ISSUES & NOTES
====================

Date Anomalies:
⚠ "ca. 1981–1640 B.C." - Appears to be data entry error (dates reversed)
⚠ Mixed BC/BCE notation - Museum uses both conventions
⚠ Some approximate dates may represent scholarly debate

Empty Titles:
ℹ Approximately 15% of records have no title - this is normal for certain catalog items
ℹ These are typically reference objects without formal names

Long Titles:
ℹ Some titles are 200+ characters (full descriptions)
ℹ Include original language, publication info, and context
ℹ Best displayed with text wrapping enabled

Special Characters:
✓ Em dashes (–) for date ranges (not hyphens)
✓ International characters (accents, Chinese, Japanese)
✓ HTML entities removed in standardized version
✓ UTF-8 encoding throughout
