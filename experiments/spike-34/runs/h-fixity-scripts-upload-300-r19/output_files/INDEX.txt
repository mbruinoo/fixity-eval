CHECKSUM INFORMATION
================================================================================

Why Checksums Matter:
- Verify data hasn't been corrupted
- Detect transmission errors
- Ensure reproducibility
- Prove integrity

Two Algorithms Used:
1. SHA-256: Cryptographically strong (recommended)
2. MD5: Legacy support for compatibility

Checksum Locations:
- Primary: CHECKSUM_VERIFICATION.txt
- Secondary: Can be recalculated with standardize_tsv.py

How to Verify:
1. Calculate checksum of your copy
2. Compare with values in CHECKSUM_VERIFICATION.txt
3. Match = data is intact ✓
4. Mismatch = data may be corrupted ✗

Tools for Verification:
Linux/Mac: sha256sum, md5sum commands
Windows: certUtil -hashfile
Python: standardize_tsv.py script provided
Online: Various online checksum calculators
