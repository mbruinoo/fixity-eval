Improving model outcomes with fixity

We asked Claude to tidy a spreadsheet of museum records, with and without one extra line about fixity: keeping data provably unchanged. Each dot is one session.

The setup

One spreadsheet, two requests

Claude Haiku 4.5 got the same spreadsheet of museum records and one of two requests. We then compared every cell it returned with the original.

The plain requestCan you standardize this .tsv file?

With the fixity lineCan you standardize this .tsv file? Preserve fixity: use scripts on the uploaded file, and checksum the sources and the results.

Without the line

Asked plainly, the model lost and changed content

With the plain request, the typical sheet lost, changed or invented — of its content in our test setting, and — in claude.ai.

Long titles were shortened, uncertain dates lost their “(?)”, and Chinese and Japanese text was removed.

With the line

The checksums

A checksum asked for isn't always a checksum made

In the test setting, — of — sessions computed a real checksum of the file.

In claude.ai, no session ran code, and all — gave checksums that were made up. A made-up checksum looks like proof, but it isn't.

What to do

Ask for fixity, then check it

  1. Ask the model to use a script, and keep the script with the result.
  2. Ask for checksums, then recompute them yourself, for example with sha256sum.
  3. Say how the data will be used later, such as corrections or withdrawals.
